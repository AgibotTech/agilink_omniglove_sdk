Source material:

  BodyParts3D 4.0 OBJ hand-bone parts, selected and assembled in
  D:\OG\BP3D_hand_assembler. The exact source-file mapping and source hashes
  are recorded in retarget_report.json and in every generated OBJ header.

Modifications:

  The source meshes were translated, properly rotated (no reflection), and
  scaled along anatomical longitudinal/transverse axes to match the existing
  OmniGlove URDF link frames and segment lengths. Meshes were re-exported as
  triangulated OBJ files in metres. OmniGlove kinematic joint definitions were
  retained and are not BodyParts3D data.

  2026-09-28 v3-rollfix: corrected each long bone's palm/dorsum orientation
  with a proper 180-degree rotation about its own longitudinal axis.
